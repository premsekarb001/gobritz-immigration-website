UK Visa Categories & Routes

Overview of key points-based UK immigration routes for students, workers, and visitors.

70 Points Route

UK Student Visa

Requires a valid university CAS (50 pts), financial maintenance proof (10 pts), and English language proficiency (10 pts).

Route Details
Sponsored Route

Skilled Worker Visa

Requires a CoS from an authorized UK sponsor employer, SOC 2020 eligible job code, and meeting relevant salary thresholds.

Route Details
Post-Study Route

Graduate Visa (PSW)

Allows UK degree graduates to stay and work or look for work in the UK for up to 2 years (3 years for PhD graduates).

Consult on PSW
Short-Term Route

Standard Visitor Visa

For leisure travel, business meetings, or visiting family in the UK for up to 6 months per visit.
